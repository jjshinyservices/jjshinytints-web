FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the legal window tint limit in Florida?

Florida law (F.S. 316.2953) requires at least 28% VLT (visible light transmission) on the front windshield strip and front side windows, and at least 15% VLT on the back side windows and rear window, with a maximum of 25% reflectivity on the side windows. Medical exemptions are available with a signed physician's certificate kept in the vehicle. We keep every install within Florida's legal limits unless you provide valid exemption paperwork. (Always double-check current requirements with FLHSMV — laws can change.)

What's the difference between Ceramic and Ultra Nano Ceramic tint?

Both block up to 99% of UV rays and contain no metal, so they won't interfere with your phone, GPS, or toll transponder. Ultra Nano Ceramic uses a finer particle for noticeably better heat rejection and optical clarity — it's our premium option for the coolest, clearest ride.

We're 100% mobile — no shop to visit. We install at your home, office, or wherever is convenient, anywhere within about 35 miles of Kissimmee, FL.
