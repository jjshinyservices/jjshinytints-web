Why Tint

Why Tint Your Home or Business

☀️

UV & Heat Rejection

Block up to 99% of UV rays and reduce solar heat, keeping rooms more comfortable and easing the load on your A/C.

💡

Energy Savings

Less heat coming through the glass means your air conditioning doesn't have to work as hard, which can lower cooling costs.

🔒

Privacy & Security

Make it harder for people to see inside your home or business during the day, without blocking your view out.

✨

Glare Reduction

Cut screen and sun glare in living rooms, offices, and conference rooms — easier on the eyes, better for productivity.

🛋️

Interior Protection

Reduce UV-related fading on furniture, flooring, artwork, and merchandise near windows.

🏢

Curb Appeal

A clean, uniform tint gives homes and storefronts a more polished, modern look from the outside.

FAQ

Residential & Commercial Tinting Questions

Do you tint homes and businesses, or just cars?

Both. JJ Shiny Tints started with auto tinting and now also installs ceramic window film for homes, offices, and storefronts across Kissimmee and Central Florida.

How much does residential or commercial tinting cost?

It depends on the number of windows, glass size, and film chosen, so we don't publish a flat price list for this service. Reach out and we'll give you a free, accurate quote.

Do you work with HOAs, property managers, or apartment complexes?

Yes — we're happy to work with property managers and HOAs on multi-unit or multi-window jobs. Contact us to discuss your property.

Is residential/commercial film the same as what you use on cars?

We use ceramic window film technology for homes and businesses as well, selected for the type of glass and application. We'll recommend the right option when we quote your project.

Do you come to my home or business, or do I need to bring anything in?

We come to you. JJ Shiny Tints is 100% mobile — we install on-site at your home, office, or storefront.
