FAQ

Vinyl Wrap Questions

How much does a car wrap cost?

Price depends on the vehicle size, how much of it you want wrapped, and the film and finish you choose, so every wrap is custom quoted. Request a free quote and we'll give you an exact price.

How long does a vinyl wrap last?

With proper care, a quality wrap lasts for years. The Florida sun is tough on any finish, so garage parking and regular hand washing help it last longer. Durability also depends on the film and finish — we'll go over it with your quote.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

On factory paint in good condition, a professionally installed and removed wrap won't damage it — it actually protects the paint underneath from sun and light scratches. Repainted or damaged panels can react differently, so we inspect before we wrap.

Can I wrap a leased car?

Usually yes, because a wrap can be removed before you return the car. Check your lease terms first, and let us know so we can plan for removal.

How do I wash a wrapped car?

Hand wash is best. Avoid automatic car washes with brushes and don't pressure-wash directly at the edges. We'll give you full aftercare instructions at pick-up.

Are door jambs included?

Door jambs are optional and quoted separately. Wrapping them gives a more complete color change when the doors are open — ask us about it in your quote.

Can you wrap my car at my home?

It depends on your space. Wraps have to be installed indoors, away from wind, dust, and direct sun. If you have an enclosed garage with power and room to work around the car, we can wrap it there. Otherwise, we install it at our location in Kissimmee.

What brands of vinyl do you use?

We work with several premium wrap film brands, which gives you a wide choice of colors and finishes. We'll help you pick a film that fits your style and budget.
